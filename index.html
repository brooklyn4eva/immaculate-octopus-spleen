About

I grew up in Miami, and I have been working since I was thirteen. That habit stuck. I am a junior at the University of Miami studying Electronic Media with a minor in Motion Pictures, and broadcast production is the thing I care about most.

At UMTV I have worked as a director, associate producer, and video producer across a long list of shows. This semester I serve as Executive Producer of NewsVision, our award winning, live, flagship news program. That path led to a production internship with TODAY in New York City and my current internship at the NBC News Southern Bureau in Miami.

The plan is to earn my bachelor's degree, pursue a master's in Media Management at the University of Miami, and build a career at a company like NBC. I want to start in a News Associates or associate producer role, move to New York, and grow toward the C suite.
